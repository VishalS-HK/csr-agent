# Damaged Product Policy

*Fictional policy for a synthetic online electronics retailer. Invented for this project.*

## Section 1: Reporting damage

A customer should report an item that arrived damaged within 48 hours of delivery.

## Section 2: Information required

Before a resolution is proposed, the customer must provide:

- the order the item belongs to;
- a description of the damage; and
- whether the outer packaging was also damaged.

If any of this is missing, ask the customer for it.

## Section 3: Resolution

A damaged item is handled as a return under the Return Processing Policy. Once the return is received and inspection confirms the damage, the customer is eligible for a refund under the Refund Eligibility Policy, Section 3.

## Section 4: When to escalate

Escalate for human review when:

- the damage is reported more than 48 hours after delivery; or
- the customer's account of the damage conflicts with the inspection result.
