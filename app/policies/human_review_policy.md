# Exceptions Requiring Human Review

*Fictional policy for a synthetic online electronics retailer. Invented for this project.*

## Section 1: Always reviewed

Every refund requires approval from a support reviewer, as set out in the Refund Eligibility Policy, Section 2.

## Section 2: Escalate instead of deciding

Escalate the case to a human instead of resolving it when:

- no applicable policy can be found;
- policies or records conflict with each other;
- the customer disputes an inspection result;
- an order is delayed beyond the limits in the Shipping Delay Policy, Section 4; or
- the customer asks for something no policy allows.

## Section 3: Out of scope

Chargebacks and fraud claims are not handled through support tickets. Escalate them to a human.
