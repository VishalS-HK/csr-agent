# Refund Eligibility Policy

*Fictional policy for a synthetic online electronics retailer. Invented for this project.*

## Section 1: Scope

This policy covers refunds for orders placed with the store. All amounts are in Indian rupees (INR). Refunds are paid back to the original payment method.

## Section 2: Approval

A support reviewer must approve every refund. No refund is issued without a recorded approval for the exact refund proposed.

## Section 3: Returned items

Refunds for returned items become eligible after the return is received and inspection passes.

## Section 4: Refund amount

A refund may not exceed the amount the customer paid for the order, minus any amount already refunded.

## Section 5: Not eligible

A refund is not eligible when:

- the return has not been received;
- the returned item failed inspection; or
- the order has already been fully refunded.

## Section 6: Situations not covered

If no section of this policy or a related policy applies, the case must be escalated for human review. See the Exceptions Requiring Human Review policy.
