# Shipping Delay Policy

*Fictional policy for a synthetic online electronics retailer. Invented for this project.*

## Section 1: Estimated delivery

Every shipped order has an estimated delivery date from the shipping provider.

## Section 2: Answering order status questions

Give the customer the latest status and estimated delivery date reported by the shipping provider. Do not promise a delivery date the provider has not given.

## Section 3: Delayed orders

An order is delayed when it has not been delivered by its estimated delivery date. Tell the customer the order is delayed and share the latest tracking information.

## Section 4: Escalation

Escalate for human review when an order is more than 7 days past its estimated delivery date, or when tracking has not updated for 5 days. Do not offer a refund for an undelivered order without human review.
